Cybersecurity Posture Brief – CEO AI OS

Prepared for: Ajit | Date: 5 April 2026 | Prepared by: Clara (Director of Cybersecurity lens)

Executive Summary

The CEO AI OS has implemented foundational security controls across the STRIDE threat model and EU AI Act compliance framework. Three material gaps require immediate attention: credential exposure risk in recent code deliveries, absence of a tested lockdown protocol for CRITICAL alerts, and lack of a formal supply-chain security assessment for the MCP Supervisor Phase 1 deployment.

Current posture is adequate for controlled pilot operation but not production-ready for broader rollout without remediation of the identified gaps.

Compliance – EU AI Act & GDPR

EU AI Act Risk Tier: Limited Risk

The CEO AI OS does not perform high-risk processing (no biometric ID, no critical infrastructure control, no employment decisions). Transparency obligations are met: agents declare identity and limitations in every interaction.

GDPR Status

  • Lawful basis: Legitimate interest (executive productivity tooling for data controller)
  • Data minimisation: Applied — only flagged/unread emails and notes processed; no bulk retention
  • Retention policy: Session-only (default); kanban boards and briefs stored in /outputs/ with no automatic purge schedule — gap: no documented retention window for output artefacts
  • Subject rights: Not applicable (Ajit is the sole data subject and controller)
  • DPA with Anthropic: Status unknown — not present in provided context
  • RoPA updated: Status unknown
  • DPIA: Not required (no high-risk processing)
  • 72-hour breach notification plan: Documented in Escalation SOP (CRITICAL alert → immediate response)
Gap: No evidence of a Data Processing Agreement with Anthropic or updated Records of Processing Activities. These are mandatory even for low-risk AI systems under GDPR Article 28 and Article 30.

Vendor & Supply Chain Security

The CEO AI OS depends on:

  • Anthropic Claude API (all agents)
  • Yahoo Mail IMAP (daily-cos-kanban, flagged-email-action-centre, yahoo-email-sort)
  • MCP Supervisor (Phase 1 deployment pending)

Current State

Anthropic: No vendor security assessment on file. DPA status unknown. SOC 2 Type II certification assumed but not verified.

Yahoo Mail: App Password rotation schedule not documented. No MFA on the Yahoo account itself (MFA delegated to app password model).

MCP Supervisor: Open desk item flags "MCP Supervisor Phase 1 deploy" with no accompanying security review. Supply-chain risk not assessed.

Critical gap: MCP Supervisor deployment must not proceed without: (1) source code review or third-party audit, (2) least-privilege scoping of its access to CEO AI OS internals, (3) documented kill switch and rollback plan.
